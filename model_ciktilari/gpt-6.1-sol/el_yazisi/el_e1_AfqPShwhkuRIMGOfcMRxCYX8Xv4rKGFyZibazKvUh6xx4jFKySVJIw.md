$\operatorname{der}[P(x)] = m$

$\operatorname{der}[Q(x)] = n$

$m > n$

$$\operatorname{der}\left[P(x)\cdot Q(x)\right] = \operatorname{der}\left(x^m\cdot x^n\right) = m+n$$

$$\operatorname{der}\left[P^2(x)\cdot Q(x)\right] = \operatorname{der}\left(x^{2m}\cdot x^n\right) = 2m+n$$

$$\operatorname{der}\left[P(x)\mp Q(x)\right] = \operatorname{der}\left(x^m\pm x^n\right) = m \quad (m>n)$$

$$\operatorname{der}\left[\frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}\right] = \operatorname{der}\left(\frac{x^m}{x^n}\right) = m-n$$
