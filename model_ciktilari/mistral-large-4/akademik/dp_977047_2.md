# GİRİŞ

Hicrî fourth century and first half of fifth century, Buyids established political dominance in southern and western regions of Iran and some parts of Iraq. The emirs of the mentioned dynasty, especially Adududdevle, tried to improve political, social and cultural life in cities such as Shiraz, Rey, Isfahan in their own lands. These cities also became political centers together with Baghdad. Also, great importance was given to trade routes. The importance given to land and water trade routes brought prosperity to many cities in Buyid lands.² Buyid expansionist policies included building houses along roads to ensure travelers' comfort and conducting charitable activities. During the Buyid period, importance was given not only to commercial and economic prosperity of cities but also to social, cultural and scientific centers in cities.

Buyid political centers were located at the intersection of sciences and scientific achievements of other ethnic origins, religions, civilizations; thinkers were placed in some regions of the Islamic world. Thus, scholars like Kramer considered this period as Renaissance or revival of Iranian culture. Naturally, many branches of science, especially medicine, underwent changes in this situation. Afterwards, the establishment of new scientific centers was seen as a necessity. Financial support was needed for the administration of scientific and medical centers. From this perspective, charitable works done as waqf could be effective in the prosperity and independence of the mentioned centers.

Most of the information about Buyid waqfs is about scientific and medical waqfs in the form of Dârü'l-İlm, Dârü'l-Kütüb or Hospital. The peak of the named waqfs coincided with the development of cities during Adududdevle period. In this article, while examining the role of waqfs in charitable works and the place of scientific and medical institutions during the Buyid period; after the end of the Buyid era, the fates of the mentioned waqfs are also investigated. The reasons for the disappearance or survival of waqfs are being evaluated. Especially, types of scientific and medical waqfs have been compared. The historical approach and descriptive-analytical method based on library studies has been used in this research examining the Buyid era scientific-cultural institution and institutions anew. In addition, the analysis of the reasons affecting the rise and fall of waqfs has been made. On this subject, no independent study has been done so far. "Berresî Mesele-i Vakf der Hükûmet-i Âl-i Boveyh"³ article only counts Buyid waqfs and determines the types of waqfs in that period. However, it does not touch on the reasons for the rise and fall of waqfs after the Buyids.
