# POLINOM

der $[P(x)] = m$ &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; $m>n$

der $[Q(x)] = n$

$$der \left[ P(x) \cdot Q(x) \right] = der \left( x^m \cdot x^n \right) = m+n$$

$$der \left[ P^2(x) \cdot Q(x) \right] = der \left( x^{2m} \cdot x^n \right) = 2m+n$$

$$der \left[ P(x) \mp Q(x) \right] = der \left( x^m \pm x^n \right) = \underline{m} \ (m>n)$$

$$der \left[ \frac{P(x)}{Q(x)} \right] = der \left( \frac{x^m}{x^n} \right) = m-n$$
