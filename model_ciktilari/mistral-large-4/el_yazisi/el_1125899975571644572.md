# POLINOM

der $[P(x)] = m$ &nbsp;&nbsp;&nbsp; $m > n$

der $[Q(x)] = n$

der $[P(x) \cdot Q(x)] = \operatorname{der}(x^m \cdot x^n) = m+n$

der $[P^2(x) \cdot Q(x)] = \operatorname{der}(x^{2m} \cdot x^n) = 2m+n$

der $[P(x) + Q(x)] = \operatorname{der}(x^m + x^n) = m \ (m>n)$

der $\left[\dfrac{P(x)}{Q(x)}\right] = \operatorname{der}\left(\dfrac{x^m}{x^n}\right) = m-n$
