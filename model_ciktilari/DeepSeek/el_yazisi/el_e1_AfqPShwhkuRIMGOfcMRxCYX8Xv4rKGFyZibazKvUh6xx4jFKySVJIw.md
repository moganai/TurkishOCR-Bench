$der[P(x)] = m$  
$der[Q(x)] = n$  
$m > n$

$$der[P(x) \cdot Q(x)] = der(x^m \cdot x^n) = m+n$$

$$der[P^2(x) \cdot Q(x)] = der(x^{2m} \cdot x^n) = 2m+n$$

$$der[P(x) \mp Q(x)] = der(x^m \mp x^n) = m \quad (m>n)$$

$$der\left[\frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}\right] = der\left(\frac{x^m}{x^n}\right) = m-n$$
