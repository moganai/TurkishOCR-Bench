# POLINOM

$$\text{der}[P(x)] = m$$ $$\quad \quad \quad m > n$$
$$\text{der}[Q(x)] = n$$

$$\text{der} [ P(x) \cdot Q(x) ] = \text{der}(x^m \cdot x^n) = m+n$$
$$\text{der} [ P^2(x) \cdot Q(x) ] = \text{der}(x^{2m} \cdot x^n) = 2m+n$$
$$\text{der} [ P(x) \pm Q(x) ] = \text{der}(x^m \pm x^n) = m \quad (m > n)$$
$$\text{der} \left[ \frac{P(x)}{Q(x)} \right] = \text{der}\left(\frac{x^m}{x^n}\right) = m-n$$
