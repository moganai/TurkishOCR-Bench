der $[P(x)] = m$  
der $[Q(x)] = n$  
$m > n$

der $[P(x) \cdot Q(x)] = \text{der}(x^m \cdot x^n) = \boxed{m+n}$

der $[P^2(x) \cdot Q(x)] = \text{der}(x^{2m} \cdot x^n) = \boxed{2m+n}$

der $[P(x) \div Q(x)] = \text{der}(x^m \div x^n) = \boxed{m-n}$

der $\left[\frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}\right] = \text{der}\left(\frac{x^m}{x^n}\right) = \boxed{m-n}$
