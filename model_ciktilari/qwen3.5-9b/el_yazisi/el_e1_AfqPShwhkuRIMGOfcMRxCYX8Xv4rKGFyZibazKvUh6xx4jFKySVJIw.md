der $[P(x)] = m$  
der $[Q_1(x)] = n$  
$m > n$

der $[P(x) \cdot Q_1(x)] = \text{der}(x^m \cdot x^n) = m+n$  
der $[P^2(x) \cdot Q_1(x)] = \text{der}(x^{2m} \cdot x^n) = 2m+n$  
der $[P(x) \mp Q_1(x)] = \text{der}(x^m \pm x^n) = \frac{m}{m-n}$  
der $[\frac{P(x)}{Q_1(x)}] = \text{der}\left(\frac{x^m}{x^n}\right) = m-n$
