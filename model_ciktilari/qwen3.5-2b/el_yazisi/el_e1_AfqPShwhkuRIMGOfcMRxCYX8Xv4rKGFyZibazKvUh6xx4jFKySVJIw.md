<p>
$\operatorname{der}[P(x)] = m$
$\operatorname{der}[Q_1(x)] = n$
$m > n$
</p>
</aside>

<figure>
<p>
$\operatorname{der}[P(x) \cdot Q_1(x)] = \operatorname{der}(x^m x^n) = m+n$
</p>
</figure>

<figure>
<p>
$\operatorname{der}[P^2(x) \cdot Q_1(x)] = \operatorname{der}(x^{2m} x^n) = 2m+n$
</p>
</figure>

<figure>
<p>
$\operatorname{der}[P(x) \mp Q_1(x)] = \operatorname{der}(x^m \pm x^n) = \underline{m(m+n)}$
</p>
</figure>

<figure>
<p>
$\operatorname{der}\left[\frac{P(x)}{Q_1(x)}\right] = \operatorname{der}\left(\frac{x^m}{x^n}\right) = m-n$
</p>
</figure>
