der $[P(x)] = m$
der $[Q(x)] = n$
$m > n$

$\operatorname{der}[P(x) \cdot Q(x)] = \operatorname{der}(x^m \cdot x^n) = m+n$

$\operatorname{der}[P^2(x) \cdot Q(x)] = \operatorname{der}(x^{2m} \cdot x^n) = 2m+n$

$\operatorname{der}[P(x) \mp Q(x)] = \operatorname{der}(x^m \mp x^n) = m \quad (m>n)$

$\operatorname{der}\left[\dfrac{P(x)}{Q(x)}\right] = \operatorname{der}\left(\dfrac{x^m}{x^n}\right) = m-n$
