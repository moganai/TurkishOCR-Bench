This study examines the raids conducted against Tutrakan by Aleksandr Vasilyevich Suvorov, who had previously played a significant role in the First Partition of Poland through his victories against the Confederates and was subsequently appointed to Saltykov's command within the First Army. As a result of the attacks carried out by Suvorov in May and June 1773, the Ottoman fortifications in Tutrakan sustained considerable damage. Moreover, during the second raid, the Ottoman governor of Tutrakan, Feyzullah Pasha, was killed in action. The primary sources employed in this study consist of Russian archival documents. In addition, Ottoman chronicles have been examined to provide a comparative perspective.
