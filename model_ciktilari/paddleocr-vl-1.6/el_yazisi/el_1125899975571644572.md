## POLINOM

der  $ [P(x)] = m $

der  $ [Q(x)] = n $

 $ m > n $

der  $ [P(x), Q(x)] = \text{der}(x^m, x^n) = \frac{m+n}{m+n} $

 $ \text{der}[P^2(x), Q(x)] = \text{der}(x^{2m}, x^n) = 2m+n $

 $ \text{der}[P(x) \mp Q(x)] = \text{der}(x^m \mp x^n) = \frac{m}{m+n} $

 $ \text{der}\left\{\frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}\right\} = \text{der}\left(\frac{x^m}{x^n}\right) = m-n $
