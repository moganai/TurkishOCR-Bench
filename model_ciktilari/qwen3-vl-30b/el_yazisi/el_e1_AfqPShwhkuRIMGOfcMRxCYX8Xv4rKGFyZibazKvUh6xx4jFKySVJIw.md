# POLINOM

$\text{der}[P(x)] = m \quad \quad \quad \quad m > n$

$\text{der}[Q(x)] = n$

$\text{der}[P(x).Q(x)] = \text{der}(x^m.x^n) = m+n$

$\text{der}[P^2(x).Q(x)] = \text{der}(x^{2m}.x^n) = 2m+n$

$\text{der}[P(x) \pm Q(x)] = \text{der}(x^m \pm x^n) = \frac{m}{n} (m>n)$

$\text{der}[\frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}] = \text{der}(\frac{x^m}{x^n}) = m-n$
