# POLINOM

der $[P(x)] = m$ $m > n$

der $[Q(x)] = n$

der $[P(x) \cdot Q(x)] = \text{der}(x^m \cdot x^n) = m + n$

der $[P^2(x) \cdot Q(x)] = \text{der}(x^{2m} \cdot x^n) = 2m + n$

der $[P(x) \div Q(x)] = \text{der}(x^m \div x^n) = m (m > n)$

der $\left[\frac{P(x)}{Q(x)}\right] = \text{der}\left(\frac{x^m}{x^n}\right) = m - n$
